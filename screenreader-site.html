park The parks at a glance

Mesa Verde came first, on June 29, 1906, the first national park created to protect works of human hands rather than scenery. Black Canyon of the Gunnison and Great Sand Dunes were promoted from national monument status far more recently.

Colorado national parks by size and visitation
Park Established Area (acres) Recreation visits
Rocky Mountain Jan 26, 1915 265,461 4,171,431 (2025)
Mesa Verde Jun 29, 1906 52,485 548,477 (2021)
Great Sand Dunes Sep 24, 2004 149,028 432,498 (2025)
Black Canyon of the Gunnison Oct 21, 1999 30,750 357,069 (2023)
About these figures

A recreation visit is one person entering a park for recreational purposes, so a single traveler generates several across a multi-day trip. Visitation years differ because each park's most recently published count does. Great Sand Dunes acreage covers the national park and preserve together.
